CRITICAL FINDINGS
=================

1. SEVERE DATA LEAKAGE IN SPLIT:
   - Current window-level split has 100% patient overlap
   - All models show dramatically inflated performance on window-level split
   - XGBoost: 0.9896 (window) vs 0.7194 (patient) - 0.27 AUC drop
   - RandomForest: 0.9961 (window) vs 0.7351 (patient) - 0.26 AUC drop
   - This is the MAJOR issue - models are memorizing patient-specific patterns

2. PREPROCESSING LEAKAGE:
   - Normalization and median imputation are fit on ALL data, not just training
   - This causes train/test contamination
   - Must be fixed: fit statistics on training set only, then apply to test

3. FEATURE LEAKAGE:
   - Unit flags (Unit2_t0, Unit2_t1) are top features - potential leakage
   - HospAdmTime_t0 is also a top feature - potential time-based leakage
   - These should be excluded or carefully justified

4. OVERFITTING:
   - All models show significant overfitting on patient-level split
   - Train-test gaps > 0.05 in all cases
   - XGBoost has the worst overfitting (0.28 AUC gap, 0.80 PR-AUC gap)
   - Models need regularization or simpler architectures

5. BASELINE COMPARISON:
   - Logistic Regression (0.7645 AUC) performs similarly to complex models on patient-level split
   - This suggests the boosted models are not adding significant value
   - The high window-level performance was due to leakage, not model quality


RECOMMENDATIONS
===============

1. IMMEDIATE FIXES:
   - Change all train/test splits to patient-level (GroupShuffleSplit or GroupKFold)
   - Fix preprocessing: fit normalization/imputation on training data only
   - Exclude Unit flags and HospAdmTime from features

2. MODEL IMPROVEMENTS:
   - Add regularization to reduce overfitting
   - Consider simpler models (Logistic Regression is competitive)
   - Use proper hyperparameter tuning with patient-level CV

3. REPORTING:
   - Report PATIENT-LEVEL metrics, not window-level
   - Honest numbers: XGBoost ~0.72 AUC, not 0.99 AUC
   - The window-level numbers are inflated due to leakage and should not be reported
